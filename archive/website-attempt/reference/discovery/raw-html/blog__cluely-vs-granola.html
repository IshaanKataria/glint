Cluely vs Granola: Live AI Help vs the AI Notepad

April 21, 2026-5 min read-
Roy Lee

The two most popular bot-free notetakers are built around opposite ideas: Granola makes you a better note-taker — Cluely makes you better in the conversation itself.

Granola calls itself 'the AI notepad for back-to-back meetings.' You type rough notes during the call; Granola captures audio from your computer (no bot joins), and when the meeting ends it enhances your notes into a polished summary using the transcript. It's a genuinely elegant tool for people who like taking their own notes.

Cluely shares Granola's no-bot architecture — audio is captured on-device, nothing appears in the participant list — but flips the purpose. Instead of polishing notes afterward, Cluely helps during the meeting: live suggested answers with your screen as context, automatic notes without typing anything, and an AI-drafted follow-up email when the call ends.

Same architecture, opposite philosophy

Granola and Cluely made the same foundational choice: capture meeting audio from your computer instead of sending a bot into the call. Nothing announces itself, nothing sits in the participant list, and both work with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or anything else that plays audio through your machine.

From there they diverge completely. Granola is a notepad first — it assumes you're the note-taker and AI is your editor. You jot fragments during the call and click enhance when it's over. Cluely assumes you'd rather focus on the conversation: notes happen automatically, and the AI's real job is feeding you answers when a technical question, objection, or curveball lands mid-call.

What Granola doesn't do

Granola's in-meeting chat can answer questions about the conversation — 'what did I miss?' — but it doesn't suggest what to say next, and it has no visibility into your screen. It also generates its polished notes on demand rather than helping in the moment, keeps no audio replay to double-check a mishearing, and its transcripts don't carry named speaker labels.

Granola's free plan limits you to a rolling 30-day note history — your older notes are stored but inaccessible until you upgrade. Its Business plan is $14 per user per month with monthly billing only. Cluely's free Starter plan gives you 3 meetings with limited AI responses; Pro is $11.99/month billed annually for unlimited meetings and responses.

Which should you pick?

Choose Granola if…

  • You genuinely like taking your own notes and want AI to polish rather than replace them
  • You want a lightweight notepad with team features like shared folders and Notion/Slack/HubSpot integrations
  • You don't need help during the call itself — just clean notes afterward

Choose Cluely if…

  • You want live suggested answers during high-stakes conversations, not just better notes after
  • You want your notes fully automatic — no typing during the call
  • You want AI that can see your screen for context
  • You want AI-drafted follow-up emails and per-person meeting history
